01 / THE MODEL CASE-SOURCED

HOW MICROLOAN
WORKS.

Small loans + business training + ongoing mentorship. Groups of five women create social collateral and mutual accountability — producing a 99% repayment rate without traditional collateral.

Beyond the Loan: Why lasting change takes more than access to finance.

01

Group Formation

Five women self-select into a lending group. Social bonds create accountability stronger than any contract.

02

Financial Literacy Training

Business skills, accounting, pricing, and financial management — delivered in culturally relevant group settings.

03

Small Loan Disbursement

Graduated microloans with zero physical collateral. Group trust replaces traditional banking requirements.

04

Business Growth & Mentorship

Ongoing support as women start and grow enterprises — retail, agriculture, services. Regular mentorship visits continue.

05

Repayment & Reinvestment

99% of capital returns. Recycled into the next cycle. One dollar funds multiple women over time.

SUCCESS STORIES MALAWI

MRS ALIFOSINA
MKONDE.

Mrs Alifosina Mkonde is the proud mother of eight children, and lives in the outskirts of Lilongwe, Malawi. 11 years ago, she set up a small grocery stall selling rice, groundnuts and maize to support her family.

She dreamed that her children would get the education she never had. In reality, the family lived in extreme poverty and often went for weeks without food. Imagine the heartache of putting your children to bed hungry.

Determined to make a change, Mrs Mkonde joined a MicroLoan group, receiving extensive business training and a loan of just £13.

17 / RECOMMENDATION STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

DIVERSIFY.

Protect against geographic concentration. Build the five-source funding engine across institutional, corporate, government, private, and digital channels.

FLEXIBILISE.

Increase unrestricted funding. Prioritise donor types that provide operational flexibility: private philanthropy and digital community donors.

PARTNER.

Build long-term institutional, corporate, and philanthropic relationships. Partnership, not sponsorship. Multi-year commitments over one-time grants.

DIGITALISE.

Scale donor engagement through story-driven digital campaigns, recurring giving, and transparent impact reporting.

PROVE.

Use transparent impact reporting to differentiate MLF. The 99% repayment rate, 575,000 women served, and $166M+ disbursed are the strongest proof points in microfinance.

SCALE.

Grow toward one million women annually by 2035. Match funding growth with operational capacity — people, systems, and community infrastructure.
