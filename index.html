01 / THE MODEL CASE-SOURCED

HOW MICROLOAN
WORKS.

Small loans + business training + ongoing mentorship. Groups of five women create social collateral and mutual accountability — producing a 99% repayment rate without traditional collateral.

Beyond the Loan: Why lasting change takes more than access to finance.

01

Group Formation

Five women self-select into a lending group. Social bonds create accountability stronger than any contract.

02

Financial Literacy Training

Business skills, accounting, pricing, and financial management — delivered in culturally relevant group settings.

03

Small Loan Disbursement

Graduated microloans with zero physical collateral. Group trust replaces traditional banking requirements.

04

Business Growth & Mentorship

Ongoing support as women start and grow enterprises — retail, agriculture, services. Regular mentorship visits continue.

05

Repayment & Reinvestment

99% of capital returns. Recycled into the next cycle. One dollar funds multiple women over time.
